# Catan card inventory: what this set covers, and what the expansions add

Checked 2026-11-16. Counts from publisher rulebooks and retailer listings (sources at the end); where listings
disagree it is noted. Only cards are listed (tiles, pieces and tokens are separate projects).

## Base game: complete
| Card | Needed | In this set (v1 files) | v2 design |
|---|---|---|---|
| Resource cards (brick, lumber, wool, grain, ore) | 19 each = 95 | `catan-{brick,wood,sheep,wheat,ore}-19-cards.3mf` | done (gold standard) |
| Knight | 14 | `catan-knight-14-cards.3mf` | done |
| Victory Point | 5 | `catan-victory-point-5-cards.3mf` | done |
| Road Building | 2 | `catan-road-building-2-cards.3mf` | done |
| Year of Plenty | 2 | `catan-year-of-plenty-2-cards.3mf` | done |
| Monopoly | 2 | `catan-monopoly-2-cards.3mf` | done |
| Building Costs | 4 | `catan-special-cards-6.3mf` | done |
| Longest Road, Largest Army | 1 each | `catan-special-cards-6.3mf` | done |

## Missing: 5-6 Player Extension (base game)
No new designs needed - only more copies of cards already designed:
- 25 resource cards (5 of each) -> 24 of each in total
- 9 development cards: 6 Knight, 1 Road Building, 1 Year of Plenty, 1 Monopoly
- 2 Building Costs cards (most listings; a few omit them)

## Missing: Seafarers
- Seafarers is mostly tiles, ships and tokens. The only card-level change is that ships (1 lumber + 1 wool) need to
  appear on Building Costs. Some editions ship new Building Costs cards with the ship row; listings are unclear,
  check your box. Design work: a ship piece icon + a 5th row on Building Costs (or a Seafarers version of the card).

## Missing: Cities & Knights (new card types, most design work)
- 36 commodity cards: 12 paper (forest), 12 cloth (pasture), 12 coin (mountains) - 3 new "resource-like" icons
- 54 progress cards in 3 decks of 18: trade (yellow back), politics (blue back), science (green back) -
  many different cards with specific effects, so these need text or a legend; 3 new backs
- 6 "Defender of Catan" victory point cards
- (4 city-improvement flip charts - not cards, but printable)
- C&K replaces the development cards, Largest Army and the base Building Costs cards (it has its own costs).
- C&K 5-6 extension: more commodity cards (18 in one listing) and Defender cards (counts vary by listing).

## Missing: Traders & Barbarians
- 37 event cards (36 + "New Year") for the event-deck variant that replaces dice - each has its own text effect
- "Harbormaster" special victory point card (2 VP, most harbour points)
- Scenario-specific cards/tokens vary by edition.

## Missing: Explorers & Pirates
- 3 mission cards, 3 mission victory point cards, 4 new Building Costs cards (one 2020 listing counts these slightly
  differently). Uses the base resource cards but no development cards.

## Suggested order (most value for least new design)
1. 5-6 Player Extension cards - only copies of existing designs (plus 2 Building Costs).
2. Harbormaster card - one card, fits the trophy/table-card style.
3. Seafarers Building Costs (ship row).
4. Cities & Knights commodities (3 icons in the resource style) and Defender of Catan; progress cards last (they need text).
5. Traders & Barbarians event cards and Explorers & Pirates cards only if you play those.
